## Featured Projects

### SaaS Growth & Product Strategy

**SQL | Snowflake | Python**

* **Objective:** Analyzed customer, subscription, and product performance as part of a team capstone focused on identifying opportunities to reignite growth for a five-product SaaS business.
* **My Contribution:** Led the **Growth Strategy** and **Product Portfolio Strategy** workstreams, focusing on acquisition vs. expansion, customer retention, product growth, renewal, engagement, and portfolio positioning.
* **Key Results:**

  * Identified a growing role for customer expansion as new customer acquisition weakened, with Expansion ARR increasing from approximately **1.5% to 16.3% of positive ARR growth**.
  * Found that expanded customers averaged **4.15 active years vs. 3.17 years** for non-expanded customers and had **14.2% churn vs. 41.2%**, supporting expansion as an opportunity to increase long-term customer value.
  * Evaluated all five products across **ARR growth, renewal, engagement, and co-subscription behavior** to identify differences in portfolio performance.
* **Recommendation:** Recommended greater emphasis on **customer retention and expansion while maintaining targeted acquisition**, alongside differentiated investment, retention, and repositioning strategies by product.
